# Privacy Policy for RefLoop

*Last updated: August 15, 2026*

RefLoop ("we", "our", or "extension") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes how RefLoop handles your information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **Local Storage Only**: RefLoop processes and stores all your job application pipelines, referral templates, outreach notes, and settings locally on your personal device using Chrome's secure `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **No External Data Transmission**: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal data, job search history, or outreach messages on any external servers or third-party databases.
- **Website Content**: When you visit supported job board pages (such as LinkedIn), RefLoop extracts public job details (such as job title, company name, and job description) strictly upon your action to help you organize your application pipeline.

---

## 2. Permissions Usage
- **ActiveTab, Scripting & Host Permissions (`*://*.linkedin.com/*`)**: Used solely to render the floating referral helper widget and parse job details on active job posting pages.
- **ClipboardWrite**: Allows you to copy generated referral outreach messages and email pitches directly to your clipboard with a single click.
- **Downloads**: Used when you explicitly request to export your job tracking data and templates into local JSON/CSV backup files.
- **Notifications & Alarms**: Used to trigger local desktop reminders for scheduled recruiter and referral follow-ups.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Protection
- We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or transfer any user data to third parties or advertising networks.
- We do not use user data for creditworthiness, lending, or personalized advertising.
- RefLoop operates in full compliance with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the Limited Use requirements.
